    Section 2.1 Giordano’s-style deep dish pizza

    IMAGE TO COME
    🔗
    1. Prepare pizza crust and sauce according to recipes below.
      🔗
      🔗
    2. After the dough rise, punch and let the dough relax for 10 minutes or so to let the gluten relax.
      🔗
      🔗
    3. Now you can either roll it out with a rolling pin, or stretch it by hand into a circle, and drape it into a cast-iron pan, with crust coming up the sides of the pan and pinched over the pan edge. Do not let the dough rise again in the pan. Stab all over with a fork to help prevent it from bubbling during the bake. At this point, you can either par-bake the crust for 10 minutes in a 450 degree oven.
      🔗
      🔗
    4. Add toppings/fillings:
      • Pepperoni and sausage: Place a layer of pepperoni followed by a layer of shredded mozzarella, then shredded cheddar before repeating these layers. Then, spread a layer of pizza sauce over the top and small balls of raw italian sausage.
        🔗
        🔗
      • Veggie: Cover the crust with a thin layer of shredded mozzarella followed by spinach and a thicker layer of mozzarella. Add one more layer each of spinach and mozzarella. Cover with a layer of pizza sauce. Top with chopped broccoli, red and orange bell peppers, and scallion whites. Reserve the scallion greens for topping the pizza after the bake and before serving.
        🔗
        🔗
      🔗
      🔗
    5. Bake at 450 for 30 minutes --less if par-baked-- until done. Less also if you cook it in a convection oven. The finished pizza crust should be golden brown.
      🔗
      🔗

    Subsection 2.1.1 Giordano’s-style pizza crust

    Source: Buzz (pizzamaking.com forums)

    🔗

    Ingredients.

    🔗
    Yield: One 9"-10" deep dish pizza
    🔗
    All-purpose flour
    🔗
    2 cups
    🔗
    Yeast
    🔗
    1 heaping teaspoon
    🔗
    Water
    🔗
    1/2 cup plus 2 tablespoons of water
    🔗
    Sugar
    🔗
    1 teaspoon
    🔗
    Kosher salt
    🔗
    1 teaspoon
    🔗
    Canola oil
    🔗
    5 tablespoons
    🔗
    Extra light olive oil
    🔗
    1 tablespoon
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    Steps.

    1. Proof yeast with a little sugar in water. Mix flour, salt, and sugar in bowl. Add proofed yeast mixture, then oil, mix with a spoon just until it comes together (about 10 seconds). Transfer to kneading board and shape into a rough ball--it should come together right away. Knead by hand for exactly 2 minutes--it should be a nice, pliable ball of dough.
      🔗
      🔗
    2. Let rise at room temperature (I put mine in the oven and give it a short blast of 250 heat--a minute or so--once every 2 hours) for 8-10 hours. The dough will not rise as much as bread dough (which is kneaded longer). After the rise, punch and let the dough relax for 10 minutes or so to let the gluten relax.
